## Video Overview

In this discussion hosted by Dreamdata.io, Richard van der Blom shares proven strategies for using LinkedIn as a social selling and relationship-building platform. Drawing on his extensive research into LinkedIn's algorithm and user behavior, Richard explains how sales professionals, marketers, and founders can use content, engagement, and networking to build trust and influence buying decisions.

The conversation focuses on social selling, LinkedIn content strategy, buyer behavior, personal branding, relationship building, and the role of trust in modern B2B sales. Richard argues that LinkedIn success is no longer about sending large volumes of connection requests or sales messages but about consistently creating value and building meaningful professional relationships.

The key theme throughout the discussion is that social selling works best when professionals focus on helping and educating their audience rather than directly selling to them.

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Social Selling Is About Building Trust

Richard emphasizes that social selling is often misunderstood as a direct selling tactic.

Instead:

- Trust comes first.
- Relationships come before transactions.
- Buyers engage with people they recognize and respect.

Key Lesson:

The objective is not to sell immediately but to build familiarity and credibility over time.

---

### 2. Personal Branding Influences Buying Decisions

One of the strongest themes discussed is the growing importance of personal brands in B2B buying journeys.

Key Lesson:

- People trust people more than logos.
- Individual expertise creates credibility.
- Consistent visibility improves recognition.

Professionals who share insights regularly are more likely to influence future purchase decisions.

---

### 3. Content Creates Opportunities for Conversations

Richard explains that content should not be viewed solely as a broadcasting tool.

Instead, content should:

- Start discussions.
- Encourage engagement.
- Create networking opportunities.

Key Lesson:

The best content creates conversations that lead to relationships rather than simply generating impressions.

---

### 4. Consistency Outperforms Short-Term Activity

Many LinkedIn users expect immediate results from posting or networking efforts.

Richard argues that successful social selling requires:

- Long-term commitment.
- Consistent visibility.
- Regular engagement.

Key Lesson:

Trust compounds over time, and relationship-building is a long-term process.

---

### 5. Engagement Is More Important Than Reach

The discussion highlights the difference between visibility and meaningful interaction.

Key Lesson:

- A smaller engaged audience often outperforms a large passive audience.
- Meaningful comments create stronger relationships.
- Engagement quality matters more than vanity metrics.

Professionals should focus on building genuine interactions rather than chasing reach alone.

---

## Relevance to LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy

This video directly supports LinkedIn content strategy research because it demonstrates how content can be used as a tool for trust-building and relationship development.

Many successful LinkedIn creators:

- Prioritize audience relationships.
- Share educational content consistently.
- Focus on engagement rather than promotion.
- Build authority through expertise.
- Create conversations rather than advertisements.

These patterns appear repeatedly among top-performing B2B SaaS creators on LinkedIn.
