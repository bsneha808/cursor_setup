## Video Overview

In this video, Lara Acosta breaks down how the LinkedIn algorithm works in 2026 and shares practical insights backed by platform observations, creator experiences, and engagement data. The discussion focuses on content distribution, engagement signals, audience retention, posting behavior, and the factors that influence reach on LinkedIn.

Rather than relying on outdated myths or viral hacks, Lara explains the behaviors that consistently help creators and professionals increase visibility on the platform. The video emphasizes that LinkedIn rewards content that generates meaningful engagement and keeps users active on the platform.

The key theme throughout the discussion is that sustainable LinkedIn growth comes from creating valuable content that encourages genuine audience interaction rather than chasing algorithm tricks.

---

## Key Insights

### 1. The Algorithm Prioritizes Meaningful Engagement

Lara explains that LinkedIn increasingly focuses on the quality of engagement rather than raw engagement volume.

Key Lesson:

- Meaningful conversations outperform superficial reactions.
- Comments carry more weight than passive engagement.
- Discussions signal content relevance.

Posts that encourage thoughtful responses are more likely to receive broader distribution.

---

### 2. Early Engagement Influences Reach

One of the major factors discussed is the importance of engagement during the initial distribution phase.

Key Lesson:

- Early audience interaction matters.
- Strong initial engagement can increase visibility.
- Community participation supports distribution.

The first wave of engagement helps LinkedIn determine whether content should be shown to a wider audience.

---

### 3. Dwell Time Remains an Important Signal

Lara highlights the importance of keeping users engaged with content for longer periods.

Key Lesson:

- Content should hold attention.
- Readable formatting improves retention.
- Valuable insights encourage deeper consumption.

Posts that keep users engaged send positive quality signals to the platform.

---

### 4. Consistency Outperforms Virality

The discussion challenges the belief that creators need viral posts to succeed.

Key Lesson:

- Consistent publishing builds momentum.
- Regular audience engagement compounds over time.
- Sustainable growth is more reliable than occasional virality.

Creators who post consistently often outperform those who rely on sporadic viral success.

---

### 5. Audience Relevance Drives Performance

Lara emphasizes that content should be tailored to a specific audience rather than attempting to appeal to everyone.

Key Lesson:

- Niche content often performs better.
- Audience understanding improves engagement.
- Relevance increases distribution potential.

The algorithm favors content that resonates strongly with a defined audience segment.
